Clubs: Complete Sets Make It Easy

You don't need to piece together a bag. Complete sets include driver, fairway woods, hybrids, irons, wedges, putter, and a stand bag — everything you need in one box. See our hands-on golf cart and gear reviews for products we've tested.

Balls: Bring More Than You Think

Plan on 9–12 balls per round, more if you play near water or thick rough. Premium balls feel great, but mid-range options offer the best value for most golfers.

Gloves, Tees, and the Small Stuff

A quality glove improves grip and prevents blisters. Carry a pocketful of tees, plus a divot tool and ball marker for the green. These small items cost little and make every round smoother.

Bags: Stand Bags for Walkers, Cart Bags for Riders

A stand bag is the most versatile choice — it stands on its own at the range and fits on a cart. If you ride most rounds, a cart bag with full-length dividers keeps clubs organized and protected.

Comfort Gear

Golf shoes with soft spikes, moisture-wicking shirts, and a rain layer round out the kit. Sunscreen and a hat are non-negotiable on the Emerald Coast, where the Florida sun is strong most of the year.
