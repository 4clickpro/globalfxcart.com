Quick Answer

The best golf cart routes on the Emerald Coast run along Scenic Gulf Drive in Miramar Beach, the Destin Harbor Boardwalk, Henderson Beach State Park, and the beachfront roads between the two towns. A street-legal golf cart reaches all of them easily — and your rental from $99/day gets delivered to your door so you can start cruising right away.

Route 1: Scenic Gulf Drive, Miramar Beach

The classic Emerald Coast cruise. Scenic Gulf Drive hugs the beach with restaurants, ice cream shops, and beach accesses every few blocks. Park the cart in dedicated cart parking, walk the sand, then continue toward Destin.

Route 2: Henderson Beach State Park

A short ride from Miramar Beach, Henderson Beach State Park offers boardwalk trails and one of the best beaches in the area. Cart parking is close, and the ride there is half the fun.

Route 3: Destin Harbor Boardwalk

Cruise to the Destin Harbor Boardwalk for waterfront dining, boat-watching, and evening lights. Golf cart parking is plentiful along the harbor, and it's an easy ride from any Destin rental property.

Route 4: Crab Island & the Waterfront

Crab Island itself is boat-only, but carts are perfect for the short drive to the marinas and boat ramps where shuttles leave — then cruise the waterfront restaurants on the way back.

Route 5: Grand Boulevard & Grand Boulevard Town Center

Between Destin and Miramar Beach, Grand Boulevard's shops, restaurants, and weekly events are an easy, family-friendly cart destination with plenty of parking.

Pro Tips for Cruising

  • Use cart parking at beach accesses; it's free and close to the sand.
